# AI 100 — Week 1: Course Overview & Orientation

Welcome to Week 1. This README mirrors the first class session and gives you a single place to track goals, topics, and quick tasks.

---

## 🎯 Objectives (this week)

- Define **what AI is (and isn’t)** in plain language.
- Distinguish **automation** from **AI** with concrete examples.
- Trace a short **history of AI** and meet key pioneers (Turing, McCarthy).
- Explain, at a high level, **how AI makes decisions** (perceptron intuition).
- Describe **what powers AI** (data + models + compute; CPUs → GPUs/TPUs/NPUs).
- Recognize **early applications**, **current use cases**, and **industry leaders**.
- Begin thinking about **ethics** and the **societal impact** of AI.

---

## 🗺️ What we’ll cover

- **What is AI? What is not AI?**  
  Intelligence that generalizes from data vs. deterministic rules.
- **Demystifying AI — Automation vs AI**  
  When a rule-based system is enough; when you need learning/adaptation.
- **ANI vs AGI**  
  Narrow systems vs. general-purpose intelligence (today: almost all ANI).
- **How AI makes a decision (perceptron)**  
  Inputs → weights → bias → thresholded output (Yes/No).
- **History & evolution**  
  Turing & theoretical CS; McCarthy & LISP; neural nets → ML → deep learning.
- **Chips & accelerators**  
  Why parallelism matters; GPUs/TPUs/NPUs for training & inference.
- **Applications & drivers**  
  Vision, speech, recommendation, search, anomaly detection; data, IoT, 5G, big data.
- **Ethics & social impact (preview)**  
  Bias, transparency, safety, privacy, access.
