# 🗒️ Home Lab Journal

> Working notebook for documenting home lab work — the messy side of the lab before it becomes polished documentation.
> Consolidated into the CyberSecurity Lab Vault from the former `nextlayersec-notes` repository.

---

## 📚 Purpose

This is the **lab journal** — where raw notes and experiments from the home lab environment get captured. It's focused only on **lab-related work**, not school or certification study.

Recorded here:
- What was tested and why
- Issues, errors, and troubleshooting steps
- Quick configuration notes & scratch logs
- Lessons learned before they get promoted into the polished [`Home-Lab/`](../README.md) documentation

---

## 🗂️ Suggested Structure

- **firewall/** → OPNsense / pfSense configs & troubleshooting notes
- **switch/** → VLAN segmentation & port security notes
- **defender/** → Microsoft Defender for Business logs & configs
- **cloudflare/** → DNS filtering & WAF notes
- **siem/** → Wazuh / Graylog setup & tuning notes
- **experiments/** → Anything experimental or in-progress

---

## 🚦 Scope

- ✅ Raw, unpolished lab notes
- ✅ Scratch configs, errors, troubleshooting logs
- ✅ Draft material that later moves into [`Home-Lab/`](../README.md)
- ❌ Not certification prep or coursework → see [`Notes/`](../../Notes/README.md)
- ❌ Not polished playbooks/documentation → see [nextlayersec-guides](https://github.com/Blackvectra/nextlayersec-guides)
